XFOIL polar cache for the NREL S809 — the project's primary airfoil (see
PROJECT_PLAN.md and the 2026-07-04 journal entry for why S809 rather than NACA
4412). One CSV per Reynolds number, columns `alpha,cl,cd,cm`, alpha −8° to +18°
on a 0.5° grid. Re = 100k–1.3M in 6 steps up to 500k then 100k steps to 1.3M
(100k, 150k, 200k, 300k, 400k, 500k, 600k, 700k, 800k, 900k, 1.0M, 1.1M, 1.2M,
1.3M) — extended from the original 100k–500k on 2026-07-28 to bracket the real
NREL Phase VI rotor's chord-based station Reynolds numbers (~525k–1.31M across
the Sequence S 5–25 m/s wind-speed sweep), which the original range did not
cover — see the 2026-07-28 journal entry.

Generated by `python -m xfoil.build_polar_cache s809` (run from `src/`);
validated by `python -m validation.validate_polars s809` (currently 4/5 — see
the 2026-07-28 entry for the one residual check).

**These are built at `Ncrit = 5`, not XFOIL's default 9.** That is deliberate.
At Ncrit=9 (clean wind tunnel) the 21%-thick S809 forms a long laminar
separation bubble at Re ≤ 150k, and when it bursts XFOIL settles onto a
separated solution branch — the previous cache had Cl going *negative* at
+3° incidence and Cl(0) at 0.41 against a published ~0.15. Ncrit=5 represents
the freestream turbulence a turbine actually operates in (atmospheric boundary
layer, Ncrit ≈ 4–7) and suppresses the bubble. Full reasoning, the before/after
numbers, and the alternatives that were tried and rejected are in the
2026-07-26 journal entry.

Consequence to keep in mind: this cache is **not** directly comparable to the
Somers/Delft/OSU/CSU S809 tunnel data, which is Ncrit≈9 (and at Re ≥ 1e6,
outside this range anyway).

Known residuals: a handful of Reynolds numbers are missing a single interior
alpha the gap-fill retry could not converge (Re=100k at 13.0°; Re=600k at
16.5°; Re=700k at 12.0°; Re=800k at 16.5°; Re=1.0M at 7.5°, 16.0° and 17.5°;
Re=1.2M at 15.0°). `PolarLookup` interpolates across each; `validate_polars.py`
check 1 confirms every one agrees with a local quadratic fit to within 0.0011
in Cl. `bem.airfoil.S809Polar` falls back the same way it already did for the
low-Re gaps (step alpha inward until a converged point is found).

Check 5 (Reynolds-trend monotonicity) is the one check that does not pass at
the full 100k–1.3M range: max L/D dips ~1.4–1.7% at two points in the upper
half of the range (Re=800k and Re=1.2M) before continuing to rise. Cl_max,
Cd_min and Cl(0) are all still monotonic/near-constant as expected — this is
noise in an increasingly flat quantity at high Re, not the order-of-magnitude,
sign-flipping defect Ncrit=9 produced at low Re (2026-07-26 entry). See the
2026-07-28 journal entry.
